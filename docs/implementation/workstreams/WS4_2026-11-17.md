## Pre-existing unit failure (MARKET)

`tests/unit/marketing-workareas.test.ts` — "searches segment rule details without changing source record order".

- Root cause: **the test fixture was wrong, not the search.** The shared `makeRecord` helper stamped `summary: "Trade audience"` on every record, so "Newsletter buyers" carried the word "trade" in its summary and correctly matched `TRADE`. `searchAudienceSegments` searches the title, the summary (shown as the card subtitle) and the rule details — all user-visible text — and has not changed since it was written.
- Fix: `makeRecord` takes a per-record summary; the two segments now have distinct summaries. Assertions were strengthened, not weakened: the original expectations are unchanged, and two more check summary matching ("email readers") and exclusion matching ("suppressed").
- Status: Verified (3/3 in that file).

### ARL-BOOK-002 / 003 / 005 — calendar, booking detail, staff & resources
- Capacity per resource: editable on the resource editor (1–200), shown on resource cards and the availability grid. The conflict check counts concurrent seats up to capacity at the peak; capacity 1 keeps the shared domain rule. Utilisation is measured in seat-minutes.
- New bookings: places field when capacity > 1 (with seats already taken in the window), past starts refused.
- Cancel: a form with required reason (Other needs a note), notice-period status, policy fee and an explicit recorded waiver; available on the calendar (selected) and on the booking detail. History message records the reason.
- Reschedule: required reason, refuses past, reversed, blacked-out and over-capacity slots, warns outside working hours, refuses an unchanged time; records previous start, move count, reason, who and when.
- Tests: `tests/unit/ws4-book-availability.test.ts` (6), e2e `BOOK cancellation and moves` (3).
- Status: **Verified**. Gaps: the week/day grid draws overlapping bookings on a multi-capacity resource on top of each other; the seeded "Trade sample morning" (4 of 6 seats) sits on a base-workspace resource without a capacity field, so it still reads as capacity 1 until someone edits it.
