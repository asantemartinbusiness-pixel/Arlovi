## Signatures

- **Communications: who said what to whom, and whether it actually left the browser.** Every statement about delivery is said once, where the action is (the page lede for Email, one notice in Compose / Reply / Send confirmation, one line in the channel composer, one "Not emailed" line in Publish review). Sent and Draft rows carry a "Not delivered" tag and the reader a "Local draft, not delivered" pill; a local draft is never shown as a delivery.
- **Marketing: the objective, audience and calendar, and the boundary before anything publishes.** The campaign page opens on the launch checklist (objective, audience, creative, channel, budget, schedule, plan approval, publish boundary) in the order a person checks it; the same component is the body of Launch approval.
