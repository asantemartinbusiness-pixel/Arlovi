# A2 · Procurement and Commerce · dispositions

Package A2. Worker: Sonnet. Date: 19 Nov 2026. Evidence: `shots/A2/` in the programme scratchpad.

## Signatures

- **PROC.** Procurement signature: the three-way match. Requisition, approval, order, goods receipt and invoice match are shown as one five-stage strip (`ProcChain`) on every order, receipt and requisition surface, with each stage a separate record and separate fact. Expressive colour (done / partly / blocked dots) lives only there; everything around it stays quiet.
- **COMMERCE.** Commerce signature: the product, its margin and the order fulfilment state. Product pages lead with SKU, list price, margin and on-hand in the record header; margin impact and change price are previews against the margin floors of live offers; order pages lead with fulfilment and payment state, and the refund surface is a four-stage strip whose "Money sent" stage always reads "Not possible here".

Evidence note: the inventory sample order `nl-orders-001` (ORD-300) has no lines and no payment field, so the refund and order-detail evidence uses `nl-orders-623` (ORD-404, one line, paid locally, with a return). Product evidence uses `nl-products-620` (Beacon Floor Lamp), which has cost, variants and live offers.

Disposition counts are at the foot of this file.
