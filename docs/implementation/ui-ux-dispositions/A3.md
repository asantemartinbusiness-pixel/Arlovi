# A3 · CRM and Bookings · dispositions

**Signatures.** CRM is remembered by *the relationship and the evidence behind each stage*: a stage requirement, a closure and a consent status each say whether they hold, in plain rows, and expressive colour is spent only on stage pills and consent. Bookings is remembered by *time, capacity and the resource*: seats against capacity, offered hours against booked hours, and a deposit that is shown as required, requested and received separately.

Evidence: before shots in `shots/A3/before` (baseline preview), after shots in `shots/A3/after`, survey of every overlay deep link in `shots/A3/survey` and overview sheets `shots/A3/sheet0-8.png`, `shots/A3/screens0-2.png`. Paths are relative to the scratchpad.

Tally (unique overlay entries per screen): Enhanced 4, Refined 64, Restructured 2, Verified 19.

Overlays that need a selected record show 'Select X first' when opened by deep link; they were reviewed through that guard and, where a selection exists in the flow, through the action that opens them.
