# A4 · People + Legal — dispositions

**Signatures.** People: the person and their lifecycle (hire, onboard, work, leave, offboard) with the least exposure of sensitive data. Legal: the matter, its obligations and deadlines, with privilege respected. Expressive emphasis is spent on the person as the page head (employee, candidate), on the human sign-off (payroll) and on the lifecycle/deadline order and the hold (legal).

## Cross-cutting changes
- `submitFooter` (HR and Legal): `[Primary] … [Cancel]`; in-body Row footers converted to `ModalActions`/`DrawerActions` + `OverlayActions` (confirm variant where it is a decision).
- `PeopleDialogLead`: the framed card and eyebrow path are gone; the heading stays for assistive technology only (the overlay head already names the task), description and status sit on one line.
- `PeopleHeader` (hr/kit.tsx) replaces the HR use of `OpsHeader`: no restating headline or description; only the boundary note and non-duplicated actions. Accessible name of the strip unchanged (a region named by the old title) where it still renders.
- Silent first-record fallbacks removed in Legal (sign authorization, legal hold, evidence detail, export permissions, clause inspector, redline diff, audience, publication impact, obligation quick view, reviewer picker, contract detail).

## Accessible-name changes
- `/hr/employees/:id` h1: "Employee profile" to the employee's name; "Employee profile" is now the eyebrow. The second h2 (name) and the section "Employee profile for <name>" are gone. Buttons "Employee directory", "Edit profile", "Offboard" no longer sit in the page body; Offboard is in the More actions menu with the other page actions.
- `/hr/candidates/:id` h1: "Applicant profile" to the candidate's name.
- `/legal/contracts/:id` h1: "Contract" to the contract title.
- HR header regions (formerly h2 headlines such as "People, with context.", "A controlled view of payroll runs.") no longer have headings; their sections remain regions where a note or action still renders. Payroll's strip is gone.
- Removed buttons: "Open directory", "Scheduling exceptions", "Browse departments", "Explore directory", "People overview", "Open onboarding", "Employee directory", "Open roster", "Open employees", "Employee records", "View exceptions", "Return to roster", "Team availability", "Review timesheets", "Inspect exceptions", "Open weekly roster", and the duplicates "Review people work", "Create employee record" (header), "Create a role" (header), "Launch a checklist" (header), "Request leave" (header), "Create a review" (header).
