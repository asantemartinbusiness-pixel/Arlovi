# B9 · AI Workforce (Team) + Missions — dispositions

**Signatures.** AI Workforce: the agent's contract (reporting chain, granted tools, limits) and the human approval that gates consequential work. Missions: the goal, the dependency graph, the stage and the blockers, with the pause respected. Expressive emphasis is spent on the contract's page head and the dark operating-contract stage (Team), and on the pause notice and step timeline (Missions); everything else stays quiet.
