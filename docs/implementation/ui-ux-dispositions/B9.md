# B9 · AI Workforce (Team) + Missions — dispositions

**Signatures.** AI Workforce: the agent's contract (reporting chain, granted tools, limits) and the human approval that gates consequential work. Missions: the goal, the dependency graph, the stage and the blockers, with the pause respected. Expressive emphasis is spent on the contract's page head and the dark operating-contract stage (Team), and on the pause notice and step timeline (Missions); everything else stays quiet.

**Counts (83 overlay rows):** Refined 40, Verified 35, Restructured 8.

**Evidence note.** Page screenshots (before on the baseline preview, after on the dev server, 1440 and 390) are in scratchpad `shots/B9/` (`_team_agents_nl_agents_001`, `_team_agents_new`, `_mission_missions_new`, `_mission_missions_nl_missions_001_execution`, `_mission_missions_nl_missions_001_deliverables`, `_team_agents`). The machine was at load average ~24, so the full overlay screenshot sweep stalled after six overlays; the remaining rows were verified by typecheck, lint, the registry contract test and the e2e specs listed in the report, not by image. Rows marked Verified keep their previous body because it is the content (a record, a result table, a boundary list).
