# B8 · Ask Arlovi + Company Brain — dispositions

**Signatures.** Ask Arlovi: the conversation, its sources and the exact authorisation before anything touches an external system. No model is connected, and that is said once per screen (the status strip, or the single capability line) rather than in every card. Company Brain: the source, its provenance, freshness and visibility, with corrections tracked. Expressive emphasis is spent on the authorisation sheet (what the AI employee would do, with which tools, under which human approval) and on the assertion trail (source, age, who may see it, tracked corrections); everything around it stays quiet.


**Counts (70 overlay rows):** Rebuilt 4, Refined 52, Restructured 8, Verified 6.
