# A7 · Administration and workspace shell

**Administration signature: the identity, its access, and the audit implication of every change.** Every consequential overlay now says who is affected, what they gain or lose, who asked, and what line lands in the audit trail. The statement that no client-side role preview is authentication appears once (Roles & Permissions, access preview notice) and is not repeated in overlays.

**Workspace shell signature: orientation.** Where am I (eyebrow, one title), what is waiting (the four-count strip on Home, drawn from the same feed as the header badges), where can I go (Go to, Pick up where you left off, command palette journey row).
