These are strategic UX/functional benchmark references from the original conversation, not evidence of an exhaustive comparative feature audit. Research current APIs/licenses/feature sets when implementing. The goal is professional functionality and coherent cross-app workflows—not cloning competitors' proprietary UI or trade dress.

## Research and legal rules
- Competitor names above are benchmarking references only. Do not reproduce trademarked marks, proprietary iconography or code.
- When requiring code libraries for canvas/raster/media/spreadsheet, verify license and maintenance instead of assuming every popular package is permissively usable.
- Avoid claiming actual regulated tax, payments, telecom, HR statutory payroll or external AI inference capabilities without suitable server/provider integrations.
- Every domain has a detailed page-level contract in `07_ORIGINAL_SPECIFICATIONS/Arlovi_Complete_UX_Screen_Registry.json`.
